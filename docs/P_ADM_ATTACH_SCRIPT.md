# P-ADM attach script

**Product:** P-ADM — Agent Admission / authority coverage  
**Band:** $40,000–$60,000 / year  
**When:** After L1 Acceptance Kit **PASS** (not during week one of the pilot)  
**Rule:** L1 proved the control. P-ADM **keeps** it. Declining is choosing open-loop again.

**Company:** AINav, Inc. · **Edge:** AINav.Institute

---

## 0. Before the call (2 minutes)

| Check | Ready? |
|-------|--------|
| Kit PASS date and evidence (reconstruct worked) | □ |
| Verb class locked in pilot | □ |
| Champion + economic buyer identified | □ |
| Written proposal ready (band + what’s in/out) | □ |
| Decision date proposed (within 14–45 day window) | □ |

**Planted earlier (kickoff):** *“Pilot proves; P-ADM keeps.”* — reference that; don’t introduce coverage as a surprise.

---

## 1. Open (30 seconds)

> Thanks for making time.  
> You completed the pilot: dual admission on [verb class], DecisionRecords you could reconstruct, gate green.  
> Today is not a new product pitch. It’s the decision to **keep that control covered** for the next year—or to go back to open-loop on privileged agent writes.

---

## 2. One-breath core (use always)

> You proved dual-admitted control under fixtures.  
> **P-ADM** keeps that control covered for a year—not seats, not inventory, **coverage**.  
> Declining P-ADM is choosing **open-loop** again on privileged agent writes.

---

## 3. Audience variants (pick one)

### Risk / compliance (primary)

> Under test, you can show who dual-admitted the write—principals, roles, exact action, policy digest.  
> P-ADM is how that stays an **operating control**, not a one-time lab result.  
> When an examiner or incident asks for the next write, you either have the same discipline in production—or you don’t.

### CFO / economic buyer

> You bought a fixed-fee proof in weeks.  
> P-ADM is the annual cost to **keep the loop closed**—not headcount seats, not a platform sprawl.  
> Band: **forty to sixty thousand a year**.  
> The alternative is free today and open-loop the first time an agent posts without dual admission.

### CISO / security peer

> You own agent and identity security.  
> P-ADM doesn’t replace that. It covers **effect authority** on the path we already proved: dual humans, hash-bound action, fail-closed, DecisionRecord.  
> Same plane you greenlit in the pilot—production continuity and support, not a new science project.

---

## 4. What’s included / not (say clearly)

| **P-ADM includes** | **Sold separately** |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| Authority **coverage** for the proven control plane | U-DUAL (depth roles/verbs) |
| Dual + DecisionRecord discipline on **scoped** verbs | U-SOR (production connectors) |
| Policy pin / release alignment for the covered path | L2 (one production verb project) |
| Commercial support for the control (not 24/7 SOC) | Custom IdP / inventory work |
| Stay on the proven pattern without re-buying L1 | Soft HITL “lite” |

> Coverage language—not an unlimited feature roadmap.

---

## 5. Proposal close (60 seconds)

> I’m sending a one-page proposal today:  
> - Term: 12 months  
> - Band: $40–60k (our quote: **$[X]**)  
> - Scope: verbs we proved—[list]  
> - Support and release cadence as in the SOW  
>  
> What I need from you:  
> 1. Confirm economic buyer  
> 2. Decision meeting by **[date within 14–45 days]**  
> 3. Order form / signature path—or a **written** pause accepting residual open-loop risk  

> Prefer we put $[X] on the order form this week, or schedule the decision for [date]?

---

## 6. Objection handlers

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| **“We’ll build it ourselves.”** | “You can. The pilot showed the bar: dual, hash-bound, single-use, fail-closed, reconstructable record. P-ADM is coverage and continuity on that bar—not a science fair. If you’re building, we’ll mark attach paused in writing and revisit when the next privileged verb goes live.” |
| **“We need production Dynamics first.”** | “Connector is a separate line (U-SOR). P-ADM covers the **admit** plane we proved. Wiring one SoR without coverage is how pilots die.” |
| **“Can you discount?”** | “Up to **10%** only with a **written scope cut**—not on fail-closed or dual. Doctrine isn’t on sale.” |
| **“Just give us a month of support free.”** | “Support without coverage trains the wrong habit. P-ADM is the support path. We can date the start; we don’t substitute free month for the product.” |
| **“We already have two approvers in ServiceNow.”** | “Ticket approvers aren’t bound to the exact action hash or single-use consume. The pilot showed mutation and replay fail. That’s what P-ADM keeps live.” |
| **“Come back next quarter.”** | “We can pause in writing. Clock on attach is **90 days** from kit PASS—after that we mark lost attach unless there’s new pain or a new verb.” |
| **“Add more verbs into P-ADM for free.”** | “Scoped verbs only. More depth is U-DUAL or a change order—same plane, separate line.” |

---

## 7. Timing script (internal)

```text
Day 0        Kit PASS / accept readout — plant attach, send proposal ≤14 days
Day 0–14     Written P-ADM proposal in buyer’s hands
Day 15–45    Active close (decision meeting, order form)
Day 46–90    Final convert or mark LOST ATTACH
After 90     Re-enter only with new pain / new verb — not endless nurture
```

---

## 8. Exit criteria (call is successful if)

- [ ] Buyer heard: prove → cover; declining = open-loop  
- [ ] Written proposal sent or live-walked  
- [ ] Decision date agreed **or** written pause  
- [ ] Path chosen: **P-ADM only** · **P-ADM + pack** · **pause** · **lost**  
- [ ] No unpaid production scope promised  

---

## 9. Anti-patterns (do not)

| Don’t | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Price P-ADM in week one of L1 | Undercuts proof narrative |
| Trade away dual / fail-closed | Destroys must-have |
| Free support month instead of P-ADM | Trains non-payment |
| Unlimited verbs in base coverage | Scope explosion |
| Endless “build vs buy” without written pause | Fake pipeline |

---

**Bottom line:** Attach is a **product decision**, not a favor. Kit PASS → proposal in 14 days → close or written pause inside 90 days.
